# Open FieldManual Requests

Store FieldManual ECRs that are drafts or have been submitted without active
target acknowledgement. Keep `Source lifecycle: Open`.

Move a record to `../in-progress/` only after recording an authoritative
FieldManual acknowledgement, intake ID, proposal, bug, or other handling
reference.

A source-controlled withdrawal or pre-submission supersession may instead
move directly to `../closed/` with source authority and rationale.
