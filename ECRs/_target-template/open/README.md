# Open TARGET_PROJECT Requests

Store ECRs here while they are drafts or submitted without active target
acknowledgement. Keep `Source lifecycle: Open`.

Move a record to `../in-progress/` only after recording an authoritative
target acknowledgement, intake ID, proposal, bug, or other handling
reference.

A source-controlled withdrawal or pre-submission supersession may instead
move directly to `../closed/` with source authority and rationale.
